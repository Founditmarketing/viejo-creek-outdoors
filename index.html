Who We Are!

Our family’s passion for the Llano, Texas Hill Country started 20 years ago with an invite to come hunt on a ranch near. From that point on, we spent every hunting season on many different leases. We were always working hard on making the deer herd better and then we would have to start over again somewhere else.

Viejo Creek Outdoors was started back in 2006 when my Keith Simpson was tired of switching from lease to lease with nothing to show for all of our hard work. It was then that he decided to find his own land that would be his and he could manage for decades to come.

Located in the heart of the Texas Hill County, an area known for producing large bucks, Viejo Creek is not your average hunting property. Not only do we have trophy Whitetail, but we also have Turkeys, Doves, and Exotics. We have been managing our deer herd on our low fenced ranch in the Texas Hill Country ever since. Through our Quality Deer Management practices, we’ve been able to raise a deer herd that only gets better and better every year.

Hunting License

A hunting license is required of any person, regardless of age, who hunts any animal, bird, frog or turtle in Texas (except furbearers, if the hunter possesses a trapper’s license.) See Hunting Licenses and Permits.

  • No license is required for nuisance fur-bearing animals, depredating hogs or coyotes.
  • Non-residents under 17 years of age may purchase and hunt with the Youth Hunting License.

For more information, please visit tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/hunting/.
